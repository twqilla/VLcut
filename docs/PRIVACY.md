# Privacy

VLcut is a program that runs on your computer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not
send your projects, media files or anything about you to its makers or anyone else.

## What stays on your computer
Projects, imported media, recordings, the sounds, overlays and looks the app makes, caches and settings are stored in the app's folder
on your computer (Settings shows where, and the Storage list on the start screen lets you clear caches). Speech recognition, text to
speech, the audio and picture tools and the assistant's built-in planner all run locally.

## The few times the app uses the internet
Only when you ask for something that needs it:
- **Captions:** downloads the speech program (Windows) and a speech model once, from GitHub and Hugging Face.
- **Motion blur with interpolini:** downloads that program from GitHub once, if you turn that mode on.

- **Local language model (optional):** talks to Ollama on your own computer (localhost); nothing leaves it.

Each of those services has its own privacy policy and sees your internet address when you connect to it.

## Reports
"Copy diagnostics" in Settings builds a text report (versions, graphics, tools, recent errors) with your name, folder names and file names
removed. It is copied to your clipboard; nothing is sent. You decide whether to share it.

## Your own content
You are responsible for having the right to use the videos, photos, music, voices and fonts you put into your projects, and for following
the rules of the sites you post them on. The app does not check this and does not include or download copyrighted music.
